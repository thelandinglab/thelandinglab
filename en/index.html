The method

The Decision Capture Method

Three layers of one system, built around how your buyer actually moves from problem to purchase.

01
ChatGPT · research

The solution moment

When the buyer asks "which X for Y?" or "best X for a team of Z?", your brand shows up as an option inside the AI's answer. Not as an interruption.

02
ChatGPT · validation

The reputation moment

A few days later, the same buyer comes back and asks about your brand by name. "Is it good?", "vs competitor". We keep your brand present in those answers.

03
Google · confirmation

The checkout moment

Before opening the wallet, almost every buyer runs one last branded search on Google. We own that result. No competitor sliding in at the last second.

These three layers aren't separate campaigns we stitch together. They're the same path your buyer was already walking.
