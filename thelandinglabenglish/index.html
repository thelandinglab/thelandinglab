The ten seconds

What happens right after someone clicks your link.

Someone just heard about you. A friend mentioned your name, or they found one of your projects on Instagram, or they typed something into Google and there you were. They're interested. They tap your link with a little bit of expectation already forming.

The page starts loading. And in those first few seconds, one of two things happens: what they see matches the quality they were imagining, or it falls slightly short. When it falls short, they don't reach out to tell you. They just move on. You never know they were there.

Here's what's easy to miss. This isn't about having a bad website. Your site might be perfectly fine. The thing is, "fine" and "I need to hire this person" are two very different reactions. And the professionals who seem to attract clients effortlessly almost always have the same thing going for them: there's no gap between the quality of their work and how they come across online. When there's no gap, trust forms before the first conversation even starts.
