What is the Derila Ergo Pillow?

The Derila Ergo is a contoured memory foam pillow from Derila, the brand behind the smaller original Derila pillow. The Ergo is the bigger "butterfly" version. It has a hollow in the center for your head, raised wings at the front and back, a cut-out near the shoulder, and a wider base you can rest an arm on.

The brand says the shape keeps your head, neck and shoulders aligned in any sleeping position. It also says a breathable cover helps disperse heat. The pillow is one solid piece of foam, so you cannot add or remove fill to change its height.

Is the Derila Ergo good for neck support?

Neck support is what the shape is built around. The raised front edge fills the space between your neck and the mattress, the central cradle keeps your head from rolling, and the shoulder cut-out lets side sleepers keep the neck level with the spine. Many people find a contoured pillow like this more comfortable than a flat one. It's a comfort product, not a medical device, so if you have ongoing neck pain, it's worth talking to a doctor or physical therapist too.
